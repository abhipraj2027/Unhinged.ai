Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What we collect

Email address (to track your scans and manage your account), password (bcrypt-hashed, never stored in plain text), and scan count. That's it.

What we DO NOT collect or store

We DO NOT store the content of your emails. Your email text is sent to AI providers (Groq/Anthropic/OpenAI) for analysis and immediately discarded. We never see, read, log, or keep your email content. The AI response (score, roast, rewrite) is sent to your browser and not stored on our servers.

Chrome Extension

The UnHinged Chrome Extension only activates on Gmail (mail.google.com). It does not access, read, or store any of your emails. It only reads the text in the compose window when you explicitly click "Grab from Gmail compose." No background scanning, no email indexing, no data collection.

Third-party services

We use Groq (AI analysis), Razorpay (payments — India), and Render (hosting). Each has their own privacy policy. We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any other third party.

Payments

All payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your card details, bank information, or UPI ID. Razorpay handles all payment data under their PCI-DSS compliance.

Cookies

We use one authentication cookie (httpOnly, secure) to keep you logged in. No tracking cookies, no analytics cookies, no third-party cookies.

Data deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from your Account page. This permanently removes your email, scan history, and team membership. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.
